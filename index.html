👨‍🍳 Workflow

1. Walk to ingredient stations → press INTERACT to pick up. Carry up to 3 ingredients at once!
2. Walk to the Assembly Table → press INTERACT to drop ALL carried ingredients onto the tray at once.
3. When the tray matches a recipe, it auto-assembles — press INTERACT again to pick up the dish.
4. Walk to the Delivery Counter → press INTERACT to serve the matching customer.
5. Use the Trash Can (next to the Assembly Table) to discard the top item or clear the tray.
